## SEC EDGAR source material

Minimized test fixtures under `tests/fixtures/sec/` are derived from publicly
accessible issuer filings on the SEC EDGAR system. The fixture manifest records
the issuer, CIK, form, accession number, filing date, source URL and checksums.

- Derived fixture PDFs are generated by this project.
- They are **not official SEC PDFs**.
- Full filings, images, logos, scripts, exhibits and signature pages are not
  included in the committed fixture set.
- LumenFin is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by the U.S.
  Securities and Exchange Commission.
- Issuer names and filing excerpts remain attributable to their respective
  sources.
